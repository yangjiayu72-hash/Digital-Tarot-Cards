# Digital Tarot Cards

An elegant, interactive digital Tarot card reading experience with smooth animations, mystical ambiance, and poetic fate interpretations.

## Features

### Card Reading Experience
- **Six Mystical Cards**: The Moon, The Star, The Lovers, The Chariot, Strength, and The High Priestess
- **Three-Card Spread**: Past, Present, and Future positions
- **Drag & Drop Interface**: Intuitive card placement with smooth animations
- **Card Flipping Animation**: Elegant reveal with soft bell sounds
- **Automatic Fate Generation**: Three card sentences combine into a unique destiny reading

### Interactive Elements
- **Card Swapping**: Click two placed cards to exchange their positions
- **Visual Feedback**:
  - Soft glow effects when cards are placed
  - Locked slot animations with pulsing light
  - Flash and shake effects when swapping cards
  - Hover effects on deck cards
- **Sound Design**: Web Audio API-generated bell chimes and completion chords

### Visual Design
- **Color Themes**: Dynamic backgrounds that reflect card emotions
  - Warm: Peachy-orange tones for passionate cards
  - Cool: Soft blue tones for hopeful cards
  - Mysterious: Purple tones for mystical cards
- **Elegant Aesthetics**: Inspired by minimalist Tarot designs
- **Responsive Layout**: Works on desktop and mobile devices

## How to Use

1. **Open `index.html`** in your web browser
2. **Drag cards** from the deck at the bottom into the three slots (Past, Present, Future)
3. **Watch the magic**: Each card flips to reveal its symbol and poetic message
4. **See your fate**: When all three slots are filled, your combined destiny appears
5. **Swap cards** (optional): Click any two placed cards to exchange them and see how your fate changes

## Technical Details

- **Pure JavaScript**: No external dependencies
- **Web Audio API**: For synthesized bell and chord sounds
- **CSS3 Animations**: Smooth transitions and 3D card flipping
- **Drag and Drop API**: Native HTML5 drag-and-drop functionality
